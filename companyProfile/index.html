About Us

Commitment to Quality and Global Trust

PT Sinar Abadi Utama is a professional wood manufacturing company dedicated to producing high-quality timber and processed wood solutions. With a strong focus on precision engineering and production efficiency, we specialize in manufacturing premium Barecore and sustainable Wood Pellets to meet large-scale industrial and renewable energy demands worldwide.
